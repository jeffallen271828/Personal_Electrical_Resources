# Chapter 4
## Schematic Capture
---
A schematic is designed to simplify a circuit and make it easier to read. If a schematic is not easy to read, it has failed it's purpose. For larger systems, hierarchical schematic are the go to. Primarily organized with a block diagram a user can click and go to the completed schematic of that system.
`If an idiot can't understand your schematic, you are the idiot`

Symbols within the actual schematic are based on ASME Y14.33-2008 and IEEE 315-1975.
