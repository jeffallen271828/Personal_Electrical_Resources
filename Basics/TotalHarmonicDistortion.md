# Total Harmonic Distortion (THD)
---
Total harmonic distortion is the measurement of the deviation of voltage or current waveforms from it's ideal sinusoidal shape. This distortion affects the power quality being applied to a system.

Within an electrical sinusoid, it is comprised of an immense amount of additive sinusoids according to Fourier. The dominant and most numerant frequencies tend to define the overall shape and output. In a grid, 60Hz dominates as much as is allowed. However there is always other frequencies present. The quantity and strength of these other simple frequencies, when summed with the desired simple frequency, result in an complex waveform alteration to the desired output. This is the origin of harmonic distortion.

When there are distortions in the input and non-linear loads on it's output, it can result in a massive decrease in the power quality of a system. Where it is defined by it's distortion factor summed with it's displacement factor

When analyzing this, we must determine what frequencies are present, and their magnitude.
